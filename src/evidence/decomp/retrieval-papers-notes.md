## Per-paper claims (one line each, sourced)
1. Zheng/Wolf/Ranganath/O'Reilly/McKee (arXiv 2503.02303v3): RL agent, PFC learns query-KEY representations for goal-relevant HPC retrieval; PFC generalizable, HPC event-specific; supplement: learned scalar BG-mimic gate (small policy net on PFC state) multiplies retrieved vector, suppresses when query-key similarity low or retrieval expected harmful (e.g. exploration). Filed as future work in their paper.
2. Savarimuthu & Ponniah — "Receive, Retain and Retrieve: Psychological and Neurobiological Perspectives on Memory Retrieval" (IDENTIFIED, paywalled; Integr Psychol Behav Sci 58:1, doi 10.1007/s12124-023-09752-5, 2023 — user's "2025 Frontiers" was slightly off; no PMC, Springer abstract only). Viewpoint: retrieval is essential for SELECTING, reactivating, stabilizing, and storing info in LTM (not just readout); covers synaptic plasticity, LTP, genetic transcription, theta. NOTE: Liu et al. (§2.3) cite this as "Savarimuthu and Ponniah, 2023" for "retrieval involves recalling stored info and accessing established traces" — consistent with a receive/retain/retrieve framing. Treat as situating-frame (abstract-level), not a load-bearing cite.
11. Liu et al. (2025, Front Cell Neurosci 19:1629796, doi:10.3389/fncel.2025.1629796 — USER-SUPPLIED PDF, full text read, raw scratch/retrieval/liu-fncel-full.txt): state-dependent memory review, neural circuits + clinical. Claims: (a) SDM = better retrieval when state matches encoding (Bower 1981); internal states anxiety/fear/depression/sleep/drugs; (b) retrieval needs fidelity of encoding discharge sequences (Vaz 2020), encoding-retrieval similarity facilitates (Ritchey 2012); state mismatch → memories overlooked/forgotten; acute stress impairs (reduced HPC+PFC, LTD, cortisol); (c) circuits: HPC/PFC/amygdala interactions + large-scale network rebalancing with arousal/affect + E/I balance + DA/NE/GABA neuromodulation; (d) BOUNDARY CONDITIONS (conclusions §7): strong external retrieval support (recognition/heavy cueing) SHRINKS state effects vs free recall; SDM weakens at ceiling (overlearned) and floor (very weak); consolidation over weeks reduces state-dependency; state must be well-differentiated; not uniform across tasks. Bench mapping: boundary (d-a) is a sharp design prediction — our adapt task is heavily cued (query carries qk/k), so carry/state effects are EXPECTED small (explains modest M-carry gains, blind gates); test U24 gate under LOW-cue support too. Mismatch→overlooked licenses the GATE-SHUTS bar (shuf = state mismatch → suppress). DA/NE/GABA → E2 momentum. E/I → U25 (#5).
3. Zhang et al. (2025, PNAS 122(44), 22 humans iEEG): hippocampal theta affords FASTER decoding of to-be-reported target as learning progresses (PFC/temporal do not); ripples (70-180 Hz) support retrieval once automaticity established. Hippocampus is the bottleneck that gets faster with practice.
4. Joensen et al. (2024, JNeurosci 44(18), bioRxiv 2023.09.15.557911): holistic neocortical reinstatement + HPC pattern-completion correlation hold both immediate AND 24h; after delay, SOME reinstatement occurs WITHOUT HPC signature — emergent non-HPC contribution, ADDITIVE not compensatory.
5. Kim & Kim (2025, PLOS Comp Bio 21(7) e1013267): spiking CA3, feature-selective inhibitory neurons via heterosynaptic E-to-I plasticity; assembly-specific inhibition beats global inhibition on overlapping engrams; DG-CA3-CA1 patterns replicate separation/completion signatures.
6. Dougherty/Patil/Duncan (bioRxiv 2025.03.28.646007, titled "On a roll"): recent NOVELTY suppresses subsequent reinstatement; mediator is recent DOPAMINERGIC (SN/VTA) activity, not cholinergic as hypothesized; dPFC activity predicts accuracy, NOT reinstatement. Retrieval is self-sustaining/neuromodulator-driven, not one-shot cue match.
7. Halpern et al. (2025, Nat Neurosci s41593-025-01884-8): spontaneous item reactivation DURING study gaps (iEEG spectral similarity) predicts later recall — rapid consolidation while still encoding.
8. To et al. (2025, Nat Commun; preprint RS-4959578): subspace iEEG — ANTERIOR hippocampus = separation, POSTERIOR = completion, during associative retrieval.
9. Cohen et al. (2025, PNAS 122(11); preprint osf hngfk): ages 4-7, separation improves with age, completion flat, the two UNRELATED (even per-event). Independent processes, separate trajectories.
10. Salan et al. (2025, Mem&Cogn 53:725-745): varied encoding contexts beat repeated same-as-test context for item recognition (4 exps); mechanism: more/varied cues raise match odds; match-reduction only partly explains it — additional mechanisms unknown.

## Bench mapping (record-search: nearest priors)
- Gate saga (U4 HEAD-FAILS+GATE-HURTS, U8 comparison-decorative, U10 split-heals-levels, U11 still-shut, U12 hard-always-picks-head, R2-AVOIDS): #1's gate is conditioned on the PFC STATE (goal representation), multiplies the retrieved vector, and shuts on low query-key similarity or expected harm. Our gates were conditioned on head OUTPUTS and trained with CE — wrong signal, wrong pressure. #2/#6/#11 add: gate conditioning should include STATE (arousal/retrieval-mode/dopamine; Liu boundary: test under LOW cue support where state effects are biggest); our carry c (M-carry HELD) is exactly a slow state channel and was never a gate input.
- SELECT wall (U22c SELECT 0/3 ~chance, U23b SELECT-HARD 0/3, soft-pool deviation filed): #5 says global inhibition fails on overlapping engrams; our scorer is global softmax over 250 slots with no assembly-specific competition. #8/#9 say separation and completion are SEPARABLE — our single head/scorer does both jobs with one knob; measure and train them separately (we already bar them separately: SELECT vs SUMMARY).
- Appetite/rehearsal (E-on-Div recall 2/2/0, A1 COMPUTE-ONLY own>rand, A2 REHEARSAL-DEAD): #6 reframes as retrieval-begets-retrieval via dopamine (success primes further retrieval); #7 says reactivate DURING encoding, not post-hoc (our A1/A2 replay was post-hoc). #3 adds: measure LATENCY/sharpening with practice, not just accuracy — all our bars are accuracy-only.
- Fold-in (U18-U23 wiring work; #True-anatomy): #1 PFC-general/HPC-specific licenses our trunk-general/slots-specific split; #4 additive-not-compensatory licenses staged fold-in over retraining (U1/U14 two-sided closure stands).
- Diet breadth: #10 (variation > match) endorses re-seeded distractors (already done) and BROAD mined sets over narrow ones — a vote for keeping U23b's wide fallback-inclusive diet as feature, not just dilution.
