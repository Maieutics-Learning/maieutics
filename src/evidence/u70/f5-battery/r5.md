# F5 barrage r5 (verbatim)

1. **Comparability Break**: The proposal uses a "per-seed MEAN" as the primary unit for verdict logic. Because the F4 baseline was a single-point estimate per seed, averaging across $k$ in F5 smooths the variance. This creates a mathematical mismatch: you are comparing a smoothed distribution (F5) against a raw distribution (F4). Any resulting $\Delta$ (dProbe/dGap) measures the effect of *averaging*, not the effect of *initialization*.

2. **Verdict-Logic Flaw**: The "F5-FIXED" claim depends on the mean passing the bar. If the lottery ceiling (MAX) is high but the mean is low, the "LOTTERY-CEILING" descriptive label is insufficient; the failure of the mean to hit the bar—despite a successful $k$—effectively proves that the current discipline is too unstable for a "FIXED" claim, rendering the mean-based verdict a deceptive metric of stability.
