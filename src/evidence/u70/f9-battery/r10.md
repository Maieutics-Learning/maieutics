# F9 barrage r10 (verbatim)

1. **Symmetry Starvation:** Halving the global scale without adjusting the learning rate (Adam FLAT 3e-4) creates a signal-to-noise mismatch. You are reducing initial variance but maintaining a fixed step size; if the "curse" is the draw pattern (ensemble), the reduced magnitude may simply delay the lock-in or mask it via slower convergence, leading to a "PERSISTS" verdict that is actually a training failure.

2. **Interpretability Gap:** The proposal fails to decouple *magnitude* from *distribution*. If seeds 2,6,7,8 still fail, you cannot distinguish between "the pattern is cursed regardless of scale" and "the scale is now too low to escape the local minima created by that pattern." Without a corresponding "louder" (e.g., 1.5x) arm, the direction is an unjustified guess, rendering the result a disguised sweep of a single point.
