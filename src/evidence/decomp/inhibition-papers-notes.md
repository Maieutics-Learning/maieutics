## Per-paper claims (one line each, sourced)
10. Benoit & Anderson (2012, Neuron, PMC3480638, full BioC read — user-found): fMRI + DCM effective connectivity, direct suppression vs thought substitution between-subjects. BOTH cause below-baseline forgetting INCLUDING cue-independent (independent-probe) forgetting = trace weakening, not association unlearning or substitute interference. Right DLPFC exerts NEGATIVE influence on hippocampus during suppression (stronger in good forgetters); best DCM family ALSO has HC→DLPFC modulation = hippocampus signals the intrusion TO DLPFC (bidirectional reactive loop: HC intrudes → DLPFC engages → HC suppressed). Substitution instead engages retrieval via a caudal-PFC → mid-VLPFC circuit that biases retrieval toward the substitute to occupy awareness.
1. Anderson, Bunce & Barbas (2016, Neurobiol. Learn. Mem., doi:10.1016/j.nlm.2015.11.008, PMC5106245, full BioC read): lateral PFC stops hippocampal retrieval via ACC (BA32) projections that terminate on inhibitory interneurons of entorhinal cortex/MTL. TWO hypotheses with different timing: **entorhinal gating = proactive** (stops retrieval before it starts), **nucleus-reuniens thalamo-hippocampal modulation = reactive** (interrupts ongoing retrieval). ACC excitatory projections onto several classes of inhibitory interneurons (primate anatomy).
2. Anderson & Hanslmayr (2014, Trends Cogn. Sci., doi:10.1016/j.tics.2014.03.002, PMC4045208, full XML read, 18 sections): motivated forgetting = lateral-PFC (DLPFC + VLPFC, often right) inhibitory control over encoding AND retrieval; suppression downregulates hippocampus AND content-specific cortex (e.g. fusiform for visual objects — later perceptual identification of suppressed objects impaired). TWO control styles with OPPOSING neural signatures: **direct suppression** (hippocampal downregulation) vs **thought substitution** (redirect to substitute thoughts, no suppression signature). **Intrusions trigger control**: hippocampal reduction tracks exclusion of intrusive memories trial-by-trial (reactive, error-driven). Applications sections (beliefs, self-image, forgiveness) = control generalizes beyond lab lists.
3. Depue (2012, Neurosci. Biobehav. Rev., filed under user's Benoit label — see correction): THREE hypotheses of retrieval inhibition: (i) diversionary attention (attend elsewhere), (ii) inhibit the specific neural representation, (iii) inhibit hippocampus/retrieval process generally. Evidence from White Bear, directed forgetting, Think/No-Think. Then THREE PFC→hippocampal-complex anatomical pathways (model §5). Maps 1:1 onto Anderson 2014's suppression-vs-substitution split: (i)≈substitution, (ii)+(iii)≈direct suppression at two granularities.
4. Penolazzi et al. (2014, J. Neurosci., doi:10.1523/jneurosci.0349-14.2014, PMC6608142, full PMC read): CAUSAL, not correlational. tDCS over right DLPFC during retrieval practice (60 humans, anodal/cathodal/sham): practiced-item benefit IDENTICAL across groups, but **cathodal abolished RIF** (sham + anodal show it). Double dissociation: facilitation of targets ≠ inhibition of competitors — separable processes, prefrontal-dependent only the latter.
5. Norman, Newman & Detre (2007, Psych. Rev., doi:10.1037/0033-295x.114.4.887, abstract only, S2 CLOSED): oscillating feedback inhibition model of RIF for semantic + episodic memory. Cycle: HIGH inhibition → only strongest (target) active → gets strengthened (incl. weak parts); MODERATE inhibition → competitors active → get WEEKENED. Explains retrieval>restudy forgetting, competitor/target-strength boundary conditions, and when RIF generalizes to independent cues vs not. Key: inhibition level is DYNAMIC (oscillates), and weakening falls on ACTIVE competitors.
6. McNab & Klingberg (2008, Nat. Neurosci., doi:10.1038/nn2024, abstract only): preparatory PFC + basal ganglia (esp. globus pallidus) activity PRECEDES irrelevant-item filtering; pallidal signal predicts how exclusively relevant items are stored (parietal WM) and tracks individual capacity. Gate is set BEFORE the stimulus from state, and its quality is measurable pre-retrieval.
7. Baier et al. (2010, PNAS, doi:10.1073/pnas.1007277107, abstract only): unilateral lesions dissociate roles — PFC lesions: spatially SPECIFIC deficit (contralesional field only, extrastriate attention attenuated) = top-down intrahemispheric modulation; BG lesions: GLOBAL deficit (both hemifields, attention intact) = general support of VWM. Two mechanisms, not one: specific modulation vs global gain.
8. Henson, Shallice & Dolan (1999, Brain, doi:10.1093/brain/122.7.1367, abstract only): right-PFC functional split — DORSAL midlateral PFC tracks contextual-monitoring demand (spatiotemporal verification), VENTRAL right PFC activates with retrieval regardless of task = retrieval per se. Monitoring is anatomically and functionally SEPARATE from retrieval: a post-retrieval verify pass.
9. Nakajima, Schmitt & Halassa (2019, Neuron, doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2019.05.026, PMC6886709, full BioC read): PFC does NOT project directly to sensory TRN; control runs PFC→BG (caudal globus pallidus input to TRN mapped by rabies tracing)→TRN→sensory thalamus. Functionally: suppresses the DISTRACTING modality (vision-vs-audition selection), sharpens discrimination (sparse response profile), suppresses background noise. Modality-specific striatal territories (audStriatum vs visStriatum terminal silencing = modality-specific deficits). Filtering happens EARLY (thalamic, pre-cortical), per-stream, and maintains sparsity.

## Bench mapping (record-search: nearest priors; nothing overturned)
- Exclusion wall (SELECT-HARD 0/3 ×9, COMPETE 0/3 ×9, GAP 0/3 ×9; U24–U30 + 6 extras): converges with Kim & Kim 2025 (assembly-specific > global) from the retrieval read-through AND Norman 2007 here: our scorer is a STATIC global softmax; both papers say competition must be per-assembly and DYNAMIC. U25 (contrastive scorer) and U30c (top-k) were static attempts — the dynamic-schedule cell is unrun.
- Gate saga (U4 HEAD-FAILS+GATE-HURTS, U8 decorative, U10 split-heals, U12 hard-always-picks, U24 GATE-FAIL): McNab says the gate is PREPARATORY (set from state before stimulus, quality measurable pre-retrieval). Our gates read head OUTPUTS post-hoc (U24 read Eqk(query) — closer, still failed 0/3 Q-USE). The pre-retrieval predictivity bar (gate state predicts select success before retrieval runs) was never measured. Baier adds: we built only global scalars; per-slot query-conditioned inhibition at scale is unrun (U29 conditioned the ENCODER, not the scorer).
- Proactive vs reactive (NEW distinction from Anderson 2016): every gate we built was proactive (pre/on-retrieval). Reactive abort — interrupt an ongoing retrieval when conflict is detected — is unrun. E2c's surprise-gate was the closest idea but its signal was degenerate by construction (mFinal 0/0/0).
- Appetite (E2 APPETITE-FAIL, E2b CONTROLS-FLAT, E2c SURPRISE-FAIL): Anderson 2014 says control is INTRUSION-TRIGGERED (reactive, error-driven), Dougherty 2025 (prior read-through) says success-gated. Both agree it is EVENT-gated, never constant. Our m was effectively constant (saturates 0.7–0.85) or degenerate-zero. The missing piece both times: a non-degenerate event detector (intrusion OR success) FIRST, appetite second.
- Substitution lane (NEW from Anderson 2014 + Depue-i): thought substitution achieves forgetting WITHOUT suppression. Our U24 after-series (i) kept/fallback split is the closest object — redirect to a fallback slot rather than suppress the winner. Never built as a first-class mechanism. STRENGTHENED by Benoit & Anderson 2012: substitution has its own circuit (cPFC→mid-VLPFC biasing retrieval toward the substitute) and produces cue-independent trace weakening — a concrete architecture (retrieval-bias head, not suppression gate) plus a cue-independence bar for any future suppression mechanism.
- Reactive loop architecture (NEW from Benoit & Anderson 2012 DCM): control is BIDIRECTIONAL — HC→DLPFC (intrusion signal) + DLPFC→HC (negative control). U33's reactive cell must therefore include BOTH directions: an intrusion detector feeding the gate, not just a gate that shuts. The failed E2c surprise-signal is exactly the missing HC→DLPFC leg.
- Monitor lane (NEW from Henson 1999): post-retrieval verification separate from retrieval. Our verdict/critic heads (U12 reports-only, F-check hollow, hv blends) judged head SELECTION, never verified retrieved CONTENT against query context. Unrun.
- Early filtering (NEW from Nakajima 2019): our funnel (U22c) filtered LATE at the scorer. Per-stream input-stage gating with sparsity maintenance is unrun. Caution: locomotive — U22c funnel PARTIAL held scores but hollow mechanism; early-gate needs its own teeth bars.
- Causal method (Penolazzi): cathodal abolishes RIF with practice benefit intact = our ablation discipline (forced-open/forced-closed) is the right method; keep it as the standard gate-teeth test. Also converges with Penolazzi + Cohen 2025 + To 2025: facilitation and inhibition are SEPARABLE — keep SELECT vs SUMMARY barred apart (already convention).
