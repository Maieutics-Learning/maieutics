NEEDS-PATCH

1. **Bimodal Blindness / Variance Leak**: Requiring both Mean $\ge X$ and Median $\ge X$ does not actually prevent "lucky tail" gaming; it merely shifts the requirement. A distribution with a massive positive outlier (the "lucky tail") and a mediocre center can still pass both bars if the outlier is large enough to pull the mean up while the median barely scrapes the threshold.

2. **Median Gaming**: Because the median is the average of the 20th and 21st sorted values, a researcher only needs 21/40 runs to pass the threshold to secure the median, regardless of how catastrophic the other 19 runs are. This allows a "brittle" model (high variance/unstable) to pass as long as the 21st run is $\ge 0.70$, potentially smuggling in the same instability the rule intended to stop.
