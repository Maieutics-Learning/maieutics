# F5 barrage r3 (verbatim)

1. **Comparability Break**: The proposal fails to account for the "k=0" replication check within the primary unit of analysis. By including k=0 (the F4 baseline) in the per-seed mean (k=0..2), the primary result is mathematically tethered to the previous version. This creates a hidden dependency: the "improvement" or "stability" being measured is diluted by the very baseline it aims to compare against, rendering the $dProbe/dGap$ delta calculations logically circular or artificially dampened.

2. **Unit-of-Analysis Error**: Averaging across restarts (k) before applying the binary Pass/Fail bars ignores the distribution of the lottery. If k=1 and k=2 are catastrophic failures, the "Mean" may still pass due to the k=0 (F4) success, masking a total collapse of the new init-regime. The verdict logic should be based on the *new* inits (k=1,2) only, or the primary unit must be the median of new inits to be interpretable.
