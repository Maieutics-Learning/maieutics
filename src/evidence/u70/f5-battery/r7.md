1. **Unit-of-Analysis Error**: The primary metric is defined as a per-seed mean over $k$ (20 units), but the verdict logic applies binary bars (probe $\ge 0.70$ AND gap $\ge 0.05$) to these means. This is a category error. The F4 bars were calibrated for single-run performance; averaging across different initializations ($\text{mean}_k$) washes out the very "lottery" variance the test intends to quantify, likely resulting in a "PERSISTS" verdict even if the lottery ceiling is high, rendering the "FIXED" claim statistically improbable but the "PARTIAL" claim uninterpretable.

2. **Comparability Break**: Comparing $\text{mean}(F5)$ to $F4$ (single run) introduces a fundamental variance mismatch. You are comparing a sample mean (lower variance) to a single-point estimate (higher variance). Any $\Delta$ measured is confounded by the reduction in variance from $k=1$ to $k=3$, not necessarily a change in model behavior.
