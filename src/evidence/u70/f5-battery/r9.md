1. **Unit-of-Analysis Error:** The "per-seed MEAN" as a primary unit is statistically invalid for this objective. Because the lottery effect is non-linear (success is binary/threshold-based), averaging $k=0..2$ masks the very variance the experiment seeks to quantify. If $k=0$ is a failure and $k=1$ is a success, the mean is a meaningless midpoint that represents neither the baseline nor the lottery ceiling.

2. **Comparability Break:** Comparing `per-seed-mean(F5)` to `F4 filed same-seed` (which is $k=0$) is a category error. You are comparing a single-point estimate (F4) to an average of three different initializations (F5). This introduces an artificial smoothing effect that renders the $\text{dProbe}/\text{dGap}$ CI meaningless; you are no longer measuring the effect of the "discipline" but the variance of the initialization.
