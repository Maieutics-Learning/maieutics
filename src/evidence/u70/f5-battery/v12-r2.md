NEEDS-PATCH

1. **Bimodal Blindness**: Requiring both Mean $\ge$ 0.70 and Median $\ge$ 0.70 does not actually prevent "lucky tail" gaming; it merely shifts the requirement to the 50th percentile. A distribution with a massive outlier (e.g., 0.99) and a cluster of failures (e.g., 0.68) can still pass if the median is nudged just above the threshold.

2. **Variance Shrinkage**: The pair fails to constrain the *spread* of the "discipline" runs. It allows for high-variance "lottery" behavior as long as the 20th/21st values are $\ge 0.70$. It doesn't prove stability, only that $\ge 50\%$ of runs passed the bar.

3. **Moved-Bar Unfairness**: By adding the Median requirement retrospectively to the "FIXED" criteria without adjusting the threshold, you risk a "Partial" verdict for a run that would have been "Fixed" under v1.1, potentially creating an arbitrary hurdle for the 20th percentile that isn't justified by the original lottery-vs-discipline hypothesis.
