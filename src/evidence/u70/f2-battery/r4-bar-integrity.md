# U70-F2 scout 4 (bar-integrity)

1. **Unfair Transfer Baseline:** The proposal compares F2-V3 (structural cut) against V2 (h-only). Because V2 lacks the z-bottleneck forcing, it possesses higher representational capacity/degrees of freedom for the same task. Comparing them on novelty transfer is a "leaky" baseline; V2's failure to transfer is guaranteed by its architecture, not by the absence of the F2 mechanism. This makes any "TRANSFER" verdict a result of structural disparity, not functional transfer.

2. **Verdict-Logic Gap:** The conjunction verdict (F2-FIXED $\land$ Phase-2 $\ge$ MIXED) fails to lock the relationship between the forcing cut and the transfer result. If F2-V3 passes Phase 1 but fails Phase 2, the "MIXED" label allows a pass despite a failure to generalize the forced representation.

3. **Bar-Integrity:** The $\ge 3/4$ regime count is a coarse heuristic that masks specific regime failures (e.g., N1 vs N3), rendering the "N2-TRANSFER" label an over-generalization.
