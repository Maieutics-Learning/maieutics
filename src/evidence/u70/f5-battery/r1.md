# F5 barrage r1 (verbatim)

1. **Comparability Break:** The "paired primary" (per-seed-mean(F5) − F4 filed same-seed) is mathematically invalid. F4 is a single-point estimate per seed; F5 is an average of three distinct initializations. Subtracting a single-point estimate from a mean of three different points introduces an init-variance offset that conflates the "lottery" effect with the "discipline" effect. You cannot isolate the lottery ceiling if the baseline (F4) is merely $k=0$.

2. **Unit-of-Analysis Error:** The primary unit is defined as "per-seed MEAN," but the verdict logic relies on "mean-probe pass." If the bars are absolute (probe $\ge 0.70$), the mean of three seeds is not a representative measure of the model's capability, but a measure of the initialization's stability. This transforms a performance benchmark into a variance test without adjusting the thresholds.
