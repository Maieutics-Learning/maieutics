## What it is
Bayesian-IL framework for LLM self-evolution: agents learn (Bayesian update
on observed d) + sample (generate from posterior). Proposition: the posterior
P_lm(h) converges to one-hot at a stationary point (local max) of the PRIOR
P0(h) subject to h ∈ Heff (IL ≈ EM argument). The interaction phase
(filter/re-rank) is selection pressure ruling out h ∉ Heff. Validated on
acronym-brainstorm iterated tasks (GPT-3.5/4, Claude-haiku, Mixtral):
posterior entropy → ~0, smaller temperature τ converges faster; interaction
variants (incl. self-refine) steer convergence toward correct+desired h.
Code: github.com/Joshua-Ren/iICL. Note: DPO amplifies prior preference
(Ren & Sutherland 2024, cited herein).

## Load-bearing quotes (from scratch/track-c/ren2024.txt)
- Convergence proposition, p.4-5: "any agent t with t > T will have P_lm(h)
  → 1(h = h_T*) where h_T* is a stationary point (e.g. a local maximum) of
  P0(h) subject to h ∈ Heff" + "By drawing parallels between IL and EM
  (expectation-maximization) algorithms, we can prove that h_T* converges to
  the h with the maximum prior probability."
- Interaction as steering, p.1/4: "This amplification process can be steered
  by introducing an interaction phase that 'filters' or 're-ranks' the
  messages generated by the agents."
- Diversity-vs-correctness solved by IL, §6: "Iterated learning can help with
  this problem by providing a posterior where P_lm(h_good) ≫ P_lm(h_bad).
  With this posterior, sampling from p(y|h,x)·P_lm(h) would be similar to
  sampling from p(y|h_good,x)." BUT "Imagine we have multiple good h where
  P0(h_g1) > P0(h_g2) > P0(h_bad), then IL will let us lose h_g2 even its
  prior is only slightly smaller" (creativity cost).
- Temperature tradeoff, §6: "we can get y = argmax p(y|h,x)·P0(h) by using an
  extremely small temperature, [but] the diversity of y provided by the
  likelihood will then disappear."
- Entropy collapse measured, §5.1/Fig.3: "H(P_lmw(h)) gradually decreases to
  almost zero as iterated learning goes on... Smaller temperature τ makes
  the convergence faster."
- Conclusion p.10: "By demonstrating that the sampling and learning
  procedures of LLMs in various algorithms can be effectively approximated by
  Bayesian inference, we successfully apply the Bayesian-IL framework to
  elucidate and steer the evolution of LLM agents."
