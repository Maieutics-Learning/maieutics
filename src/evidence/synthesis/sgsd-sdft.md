## SGSD — Skill-Conditioned Gated Self-Distillation (2605.28791)
Self-distillation for math reasoning with verifier feedback. Cold-start
skill bank from the initial policy's own traces; skill–mistake pairs
become teacher-side privileged-info blocks; a pool of skill-conditioned
teachers scores the SAME student rollout; verifier outcome validates
each teacher's polarity; gated distillation learns from informative
disagreements. Beats GRPO and naive skill-injection (AIME24/25);
competitive with answer-conditioned OPSD under weaker assumptions.
Quotes (w11-sgsd.txt):
- Recipe: "successful traces are summarized into candidate general
  skills, failed traces are summarized into candidate mistake
  patterns, and redundant candidates are merged into a compact bank."
- Pool: "skill–mistake pairs instantiate a pool of skill-conditioned
  teachers. Multiple teachers score the same rollout, the verifier
  outcome validates their polarity, and a gated distillation objective
  turns teacher-student gaps into dense supervision."
- Gate: "The loss value is near zero when teacher and student agree,
  grows for moderate disagreement, and saturates for extreme
  mismatch" — "the gate keeps medium teacher-student disagreements as
  useful dense supervision while preventing outlier logits."
- Dense-vs-sparse: "GRPO brings only limited gains, which is consistent
  with the sparsity of final-answer, trajectory-level rewards. OPSD and
  SGSD turn sparse rewards into dense token-level supervision."
Novelty delta: they [distill from a skill-conditioned multi-teacher
pool] under [single-model math RLVR + verifier]; we [fade hints across
tutor→student generations] under [tiny lookup curriculum]; claim holds
— no generations, no fading, no teaching in SGSD. So-what: F-skillbank
construction recipe, verbatim (our ledgers already hold the raw
material: killA-collapse s205/s206, memorize-lean s603, severance s608
+ success shapes).

## SDFT — Self-Distillation Bridges Distribution Gap (2402.13669)
Thesis: the distribution gap between task data and the model's own
distribution is the primary cause of forgetting/misalignment in
fine-tuning; fix by guiding fine-tuning with self-distilled data
matching the original distribution. Llama-2-chat: mitigates
catastrophic forgetting, keeps helpfulness/safety, matches/beats
vanilla fine-tuning.
Quotes (w12-sdft.txt):
- "we posit that the distribution gap between task datasets and the
  LLMs serves as the primary underlying cause."
- Fix: "guiding fine-tuning with a distilled dataset generated by the
  model itself to match its original distribution."
- Result: "SDFT effectively mitigates catastrophic forgetting while
  achieving comparable or superior performance on downstream tasks
  compared to the vanilla fine-tuning" + "maintain the helpfulness and
  safety alignment of LLMs."
Novelty delta: they [self-distill to bridge the fine-tuning gap] under
[alignment fine-tuning]; we [gate + promote to stay near the tutor
distribution] under [generational loop]; claim holds — alignment
fine-tuning, no teaching/fading/generations. So-what: names WHY ungated
chains collapse (C3) and why ours doesn't — winner-promotion + ignition
gate keep each generation near the tutor distribution. Our loop is a
distribution-gap-managed loop.
