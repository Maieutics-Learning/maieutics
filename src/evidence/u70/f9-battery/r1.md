# F9 barrage r1 (verbatim)

1. **Symmetry-Breaking Failure**: Halving the global scalar without adjusting the learning rate or providing a warming phase risks "frozen" symmetry. If the 0.5x scale pushes initial activations into the linear regime of the activation functions, the network may fail to break symmetry entirely, leading to a "pooled collapse" that is indistinguishable from a "quiet birth."

2. **Confounded Mechanism**: The proposal attributes "catastrophes" to volume (magnitude), but doesn't isolate volume from variance. By scaling all draws by 0.5, you are reducing the variance of the initial distribution. If the seeds still fail, you cannot distinguish between a "pattern of draws" (topology) and "magnitude of draws" (volume).

3. **Unjustified Direction**: "Quieter" is an assumption. Without a corresponding "Louder" (e.g., 2.0x) arm, this is a disguised single-point sweep, not a controlled experiment.
