# F5 barrage r6 (verbatim)

1. **Comparability Break**: The "Paired primary" compares $mean(F5)$ against $F4$ filed same-seed. Since $F4$ was $k=0$ only, and $F5$ is the mean of $k \in \{0, 1, 2\}$, the delta $\Delta = mean(k_{0,1,2}) - k_0$ is mathematically equivalent to $\frac{1}{3}(k_1 + k_2 - 2k_0)$. This is not a measure of "lottery noise" but a specific bias toward the average of the new inits. 

2. **Unit-of-Analysis Error**: The verdict is gated on the per-seed mean, but the "Lottery ceiling" is descriptive. If $max(k) > mean(k)$, you have a "hidden" success. By reporting the mean as the primary, you are effectively averaging a "hit" (the lottery win) with two "misses" (random inits), potentially dragging a successful seed-path below the bar. You are measuring the *average* of a lottery, which is conceptually void.
