1. **Comparability Break**: The "per-seed MEAN" primary unit is mathematically incompatible with the binary "pass/fail" bars (probe $\ge 0.70$, gap $\ge 0.05$). Because the bars are non-linear thresholds, a mean of three runs cannot be mapped to a binary pass/fail without redefining the bar as a "mean-pass." If the bar is applied to the mean, the sensitivity to the "lottery" (outliers) is dampened, rendering the "Lottery Ceiling" descriptive analysis meaningless as it operates on a different scale than the verdict logic.

2. **Unit-of-Analysis Error**: The proposal claims to isolate the "init lottery" but aggregates via mean across $k=0..2$. Averaging across disparate initializations collapses the very variance the experiment intends to measure, transforming a "lottery" study into a noisy replication of F4. You cannot claim to quantify headroom via MAX while basing the verdict on MEAN.
