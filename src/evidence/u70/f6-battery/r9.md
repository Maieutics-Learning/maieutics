# F6 barrage r9 (verbatim)

1. **Gradient Leakage via q-posterior:** The "soft teacher" reads $q$, which is the output of the encoder. By backpropagating through the softmax-jacobian path of the soft teacher, you are effectively introducing a second, smoother training signal for $z$. This is a disguised version of increasing `ctxw` (context weight); it doesn't isolate the "flip mechanism," it just changes the shape of the gradient pushing $z$.

2. **Weight Unjustification:** $w=1.0$ is arbitrary. Without a principled derivation or a controlled sweep, this is a "magic number" that could either over-power the KL-term or be negligible, making any "FIXED" result an artifact of this specific scalar rather than the architectural change.

3. **Convergence Instability:** Adding a new head with its own Adam state to a BPTT-50 carry-over chain without tuning the learning rate risks gradient spikes during the "smooth" transition, potentially masking the very lottery effects you aim to measure.
