Teaching Addressable Lookup to Small Recurrent Networks by Staged Hint Fading

Program: Teaching Small Models to Learn · Authors: TBD · Status: draft v0.13, 2026-11-10 · Code: github.com/2012dzli/Maieutics, src/tinynn · Run records: src/evidence/gseries-v2 (bench ledgers) + src/evidence/rabbithole (traces); h2/D-wave originals lost in a scratch wipe, rebuilt as v2 · Paper shelf: arXiv:2502.05171, arXiv:2512.19428, arXiv:2312.00752, arXiv:2412.06769, arXiv:2301.13196

Abstract. In this work we investigate whether a small recurrent network can be taught genuine rule-like lookup — retrieving a bound value from an arbitrary position on demand — or whether it can only memorize and shortcut. We propose staged hint fading as a transmission mechanism: a frozen hint-trained teacher supervises a hint-free student while the supervision is graduated to zero. On a bench associative-recall task with fair-slot training, attention-based small nets look up reliably (0.78–0.84 flat across positions) while geometry-only and notebook variants sit near chance. Answer-only training at h48 never ignites (ignite ⇔ Eq. 3; 0/32 across four architectures, 95% CI 0.00–0.11; more steps memorize or heuristic-lock rather than teach). But a frozen hint-trained teacher can transmit the rule to a hint-free student through staged hint fading: 5/8 students ignite with a fast fade (95% Wilson CI 0.31–0.86), and a slower fade rescues 1 of 3 failures (95% CI 0.06–0.79). Chained tutor-to-student across five generations, 35/40 ignite (G1 8/8, G2 6/8, G3 7/8, G4 8/8, G5 6/8) with best students perfect in every generation — teaching compounds, not rots. Transmission is therefore possible but fragile, and failure modes differ by seed. Our contribution is a teachability result with pre-registered bars, plus a characterization of exactly where transmission breaks (severance-fragile collapse, memorize-lean, slow-rescuable). All effect sizes are regime-bound (h48, one task family, n ≤ 8 per condition); the scaling hypothesis and cross-family transfer are TBD. Stated failure boundary: cold copying transmits nothing (0/9, 95% CI 0.00–0.33), no single fade schedule fits all seeds, and nothing is claimed beyond h48, one task family, and the schedules tested. This draft reports the first step (transmission) plus its chaining (G1→G5, §8.1); its declared endpoint is §8 — a nursery that, given a topic, creates from scratch a full model that learns it. Everything past G3 is roadmap with pre-registered bars, not results.

2 · Related work

Three lines of work converge on this paper — latent recurrence that thinks without writing (§2.1), verbalized test-time compute (§2.2), and attention-free geometry (§2.3) — on top of older recurrent priors (§2.4). Each gets a subsection; §2.5 says where we stand.

2.1 Latent recurrence: Huginn

Geiping et al. (2025) build . The macroscopic design is a triplet — prelude P embeds, core R iterates, coda C decodes — with the embedded input re-injected every step from a random start (Eq. 1):

e = P(x),   s0 ∼ N(0, σ2I),   si = R(e, si−1),   p = C(sr)  (1)

Training samples a random iteration count per forward pass with standard next-token loss and truncated backpropagation; at test time the core unrolls further than training. Two design constraints we inherit explicitly: input re-injection every step and random initialization, which the deep-thinking literature shows stabilize recurrence toward path independence (Bansal et al., 2022; Anil et al., 2022). Reported emergences include per-token adaptive compute, zero-shot KV-cache sharing, continuous chain-of-thought, self-speculative decoding (§6), and orbiting trajectories on numerical prompts (§7). Their philosophical bet is ours too: “By constructing an architecture that is compute-heavy and small in parameter count, we hope to set a strong prior towards models that solve problems by ‘thinking’ … instead of memorizing,” toward “a third axis on which to scale model performance” (§2). We test that prior at bench scale: our depth curves (§4.1) are their Fig. 1 in miniature, and our orbit visualizer (Appendix C) emulates their §7 to build intuition before touching a real net.

2.2 Verbalized test-time compute

(Wei et al., 2022) externalizes reasoning into tokens; s1 (Muennighoff et al., 2025) scales it by budget-forcing with a literal “Wait”; o1 (OpenAI, 2024) industrialized it. Against these, latent recurrence needs no bespoke long demonstrations and pays FLOPs instead of context (Geiping et al., 2025, §2). Our cost table (Appendix B) concedes the corollary: our own live demo layers refinement calls on top of an LLM and therefore pays the token cost the real architecture avoids.

2.3 De-centering attention: Grassmann flows

Zhang (2025) reinterprets self-attention as — mapping a hidden vector into a high-dimensional space of pairwise interactions — and argues opacity is structural: “a major source of large-model uninterpretability is that the tensor lifting performed by attention is mathematically non-traceable.” The proposed alternative encodes local token pairs as 2D subspaces of a Grassmann manifold Gr(2, r) via Plücker coordinates, with no attention matrix anywhere (Eq. 2):

zt = Wred ht + bred,   pij(t, Δ) = zt,i zt+Δ,j − zt,j zt+Δ,i,   1 ≤ i < j ≤ r  (2)

with multi-scale offsets Δ in (1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16), rank r = 32, and gated fusion back into the stream (in Zhang's notation ht is the stream state, Wred a learned reduction, bred its bias). A 13–18M-parameter pure-Grassmann LM lands within 10–15% of a size-matched Transformer on Wikitext-2; a Grassmann NLI head edges a Transformer head on SNLI (test 0.8538 vs 0.8511); mixing scales linearly in sequence length for fixed rank. The paper's aim is our epigraph: “Our aim is not to claim that attention is obsolete, but to de-center it. What one fundamentally needs is not attention itself, but a sufficiently expressive geometric evolution mechanism.” We take this as a design brief, not gospel (single author, weak absolute numbers, unreplicated): our wedge/geometric keys + addressing head (config M4, §3.2) is exactly “geometric features, addressing read-out,” and at h48 it beats attention (1.00 vs 0.80, §4.1). Two of the paper's own pointers shape our queue: attention maps show where, not why (§7.4 — our weight readouts carry that label), and §6.4 names global and long-range invariants as the next step — the precise door our running-mean control closes in averaged-subspace form (§4.1). 3 · Method

3.1 Notation and terms

Scale: h48 = hidden width 48, m96 = MLP width 96, d16 = attention width 16; r = unroll depth. Exams (all exact-match accuracies on disjoint-seed splits; full exam specs in §3.3): killA (held-out-line format, allowed combos), killHd (diag targets + allowed distractors), killDa (mirror), killHu (novel combos), in-dist, hard (4–6 pairs, depth curve r = 1..16). ignite ⇔ Eq. 3. Protocols: cold copy (D1, soft targets only), fade (D2+, graduated attention-supervision severance 100 → 50 → 0%). Failure signatures: severance-collapse (s42: killA 0.30 at severance), memorize-lean (killA up, Hd down; s43: 0.95/0.185), heuristic-lock (Hd up, killA down; M4 tier-2 drift 0.46 → 0.285 while Hd 0.21 → 0.565), S (selection, “S-addressing”): the addressing read-out pathway that selects the bound value at test time; S-only = selection pathway with geometric keys ablated (vs geo-only: keys with selection lesioned). fair homework = randomized target slots.

3.2 Cores (src/tinynn)

All cores share prelude → loop → coda plumbing with manual BPTT (Adam, grad-check exact to about 1e-9 before any training claim). rec/seq: pooled-MLP and gated running-state cores. attn (A0): single-head causal dot-product memory over past position states, gated retrieval, −log w(target) on the final step. geo (A1): wedge/geometric keys, offsets (1, 2), no attention. global (A2/M2): adds a running-mean channel. off2 (A3/M3/M4): doubled wedge scaffolding; M4 = off2 with no global channel (our best config). S-only: ablated student pathway probe. Query-head, gist labels, settle-training (grade every depth round), and PCGrad surgery are opt-in supervisions; every one is ablated somewhere in §4. Reproducibility and compute: CPU-only throughout, zero dependencies (plain JS, no WASM/WebGPU; runs in any modern browser). Timing probe on a 10-logical-core laptop CPU (16 GB RAM; browser exposes no model string, so hardware varies run to run — wall-clocks below are from the run ledger): geo h48, batch 16 → 10.8 ms/step, so a 3k-step seed ≈ 35 s. Counted steps in the named H2/D waves: 205k (≈ 37 min at probe rate); with the H/C/K/Phase-2/evo runs and evals, the entire program is single-digit CPU-hours on laptop-class hardware (≈0.1–0.2 kWh total, ≈0.05–0.1 kgCO₂e at average grid intensity). Pinned-cluster spec: n/a (browser-native).

3.3 Tasks, homework, exams

Associative recall over pairs (k, v) with a query marker Q: the model must emit the bound value. Training mixes short lines with 500 long gap-8 lines at randomized target slots ( — randomizing the slot, not just the length, after we caught models solving a fixed hiding spot, §6). Exams, all with disjoint seeds from training: in-dist, hard (4–6 pairs, depth curve r = 1..16), killA (held-out-line format, allowed combos), killHd (diag targets + allowed distractors — the hard direction), killDa (mirror), killHu (novel combos), gap-1/gap-20 probes, position-0..8 sweeps. All kill scores are exact-match accuracies on their exam split. killA ≥ 0.60 and killHd ≥ 0.55 — one cell alone is shortcut-compatible (high Hd with low killA = echo-the-key heuristic, §4.2). Proxy check: Spearman rank agreement between mild-200 proxy scores and full killHd across configs (bar 0.60).

ignite ⇔ killA ≥ 0.60 ∧ killHd ≥ 0.55  (3)

3.4 Distillation protocol (Wave D family)

Tutor: frozen hint-trained hybrid M4 (allowed-12 pool), load-verified at killA 1.00, Hd 0.88, Hu 1.00. Student: freshly-initialized same-arch net, same pool. soft targets only (+ entropy variants), 3k steps, fidelity read at 3k, extend arm to 6k. soft targets + attention supervision kept with probability 100% (steps 0–1k) → 50% (1–2k) → 0% (2–3k), checkpoint evals each stage. D-rate: same fade × 8 fresh seeds, ignition read at severance. D-slow: on the three D-rate failures + one ignited control. Read rules pre-registered per wave (slow-transmission vs heuristic-lock vs memorize signatures). Chaining ran as §8.1 (G1→G5, tutor-pick rule, exact-save harness).

5 · Discussion

The same arch + data that ignites hinted (M4 1.00, attention about 0.80 fair) never ignites answer-only — so arch-sufficiency is established and the residual is the signal: supervision content, not steps. Cold copying falls into the echo-heuristic attractor (fidelity never transmits); graduated severance transmits for a majority (6/10) with three distinct failure signatures — severance-fragile (collapse), memorize-prone (killA up, Hd down), and slow-rescuable. Slower fading helps one, harms one, delays one: failure modes differ by seed, so no single schedule is optimal. Winners deploy S-addressing at test time with geometric keys as training scaffolding; the global channel is exactly null (not poison — zero grads by construction); off2 doubles the wedge signal and overwhelms the attractor. Not a claim about scale (h48 only), not about language (one task family), not about drive (we build ability-to-learn; goal-generation stays outside the model).

6 · Missteps and retractions

Kept, because the log is the method — read it as the ablation record: each retraction is a control that constrains the surviving claims. Each item names what replaced it.

  • Position-fixed training taught “answer = first pair” (pos0 1.00, elsewhere chance) — every earlier 1.00 measured the hiding spot. Replaced by randomized-slot fair homework; F2/F3 re-measured honestly.
  • E1b pair-specificity. Held-out-only killH 0.00 looked like a second wall; under fair slots it was a shortcut-regime artifact (K/K2: Hd 0.79–0.88 both arches).
  • “W-term shrank” / “global noise + uniform grads.” Neither reproduced on measurement. Replaced by the (noG ≡ full; 1.2e-9 cross-run weight identity; softmax cancellation).
  • Hd/Da gloss swap. Prose labels were backwards; scripts ruled. Correct reading: novel targets hard, alien distractors harmless.
  • PCGrad +0.08. Best draw, not typical (+0.03–0.05 same-data); kept opt-in, default stays matched-data + fused.
  • B1 trains 4× faster. Browser wall clock, uninterpretable — retracted as a claim, kept as a timing anomaly.
  • nn-diff “linear” bisect. Forward-only change = built-in forward/backward mismatch; void as evidence.
  • Evo void-seed. The fair campaign re-dealt identical slices every round; prior climbs were memorization artifacts. Replaced by reseed + holdout gate; readout genes exhausted.
  • Reviewer-fed errors. Two stale-premise corrections (“A3 is best” after M4 hit 1.00; 1.2e-9 misread as magnitude) — artifact hygiene, §2 of src/tools/fresh-eyes/SKILL.md.

7 · Limitations and TBDs

Taken together, the limits bound the claim, not just the data: every positive result is conditional on h48 scale, single-digit seeds, and one task family, and the three transmission failure signatures (collapse, memorize-lean, killA-collapse) show that no single schedule generalizes even within this regime. What follows lists each limit with what would lift it.

  • Scale. Width sweep ran 2026-11-10 (proportional h/m/d scaling, same pool/fade/seeds 201–208): h24 T0 fails the bar (Hd 0.205) yet its students ignite 8/8 (Hd 0.565–1.00) — transmission works where hinted training fails. h96: T0 0.575, students 5/8 (95% CI 0.31–0.86) with two killA-collapses, a failure shape unseen at h48-G1. Rates overlap (h24/h48 8/8, h96 5/8): no scale collapse at 4× width, wider failure repertoire at the top. New hinge: other architectures. MEASURED (width sweep).
  • Seeds. 8 seeds per claim throughout the v2 chain and sweeps (; seed 7 the maximum in all four hinted arches); proportions carry 95% Wilson CIs. MEASURED (n=8 bars).
  • Transfer. Cross-domain battery ran 2026-11-10 (eval-only on saved v2 blobs, src/evidence/gseries-v2/xfer-results.json): mid-slot layout shift, gap-12 distance extrapolation, and symbol-permutation relabeling, each in A/Hd variants. Every ignited student (s202, s308, s405, s502, s606) scores 0.97–1.00 on all six — transfer holds, and students exceed T0 (0.675/0.455/0.49 on the three Hd analogues). Memorize-lean seeds fail transfer exactly (s304: 0.08–0.14 Hd analogues; s603: 0.425–0.52); the collapse (s608) is chance everywhere. Transfer tracks ignition. New hinge: cross-family transfer (new task family). MEASURED (transfer battery).
  • Generations. : teaching compounds through five handoffs — the loop is self-sustaining and best students are perfect every generation. Width sweep (h24 8/8, h96 5/8) shows no scale collapse. New hinge: other architectures. MEASURED (§8.1).
  • Mechanism. Endpoint gradient audit ran 2026-11-10 (same tutor/pool; s302-ignite vs s304-memorize-lean vs s306-near-miss; fixed-probe soft-vs-attn cosine + norms, 30-point timecourse, src/evidence/gseries-v2/snr-results.json): the igniter settles both channels (|S|~0.003, |A|~0.0004); memorize-lean leaves the attention channel hot (|A| 9× the igniter's, |S|/|A|≈1.5 — the teachers never reconciled); the near-miss settles both at a sub-bar solution. Transient: both failures blip to +0.37 alignment mid-stage-1 then flip at the fade step; the igniter stays near zero throughout. Hypothesis: memorize-lean is unreconciled teachers at severance, ignition is joint settling. n=1 per shape — multi-seed confirmation is the follow-up. MEASURED (gradient audit).
  • Related work. Pass ran 2026-11-10 (primaries: arXiv:2312.00752, 2412.06769, 2301.13196; bench baseline src/evidence/gseries-v2/ssm-baseline.json): Mamba's selectivity (input-dependent Δ/B/C) solves its copying/induction-head synthetics, but the Mamba-like bench config (selective write-gate + query-conditioned read, fair slots h48/1200) scores killA 0.465, Hd 0.00, transfer 0.00 — where attention transmits at 0.97–1.00. Input-dependent gating is not sufficient for arbitrary-slot novel-target lookup here; addressing (S-pathway) is. Coconut's continuous-thought feedback maps to our depth loop + settle-training (grade every round); no contradiction (single trajectory vs BFS noted). Looped transformers share §2.4's path-independence lineage (loop as computer vs our loop as thinker); attention's versatility matches our S-lesion result. MEASURED (related-work pass).

Appendix A · The latent-recurrence background (interactive)

From Geiping et al., sections 3–4. Three groups of transformer layers:

Prelude Pembeds tokens into latent space
e = P(x)
Recurrent core R ↺loops r times:
s(i) = R(e, s(i-1))
input e re-injected every step, random start s(0)
Coda Cdecodes last state
p = C(s_r)

Training trick: train with a random number of iterations each forward pass, standard next-token loss, no chain-of-thought data. Microscopic details that made it stable at scale: sandwich RMSNorm, RoPE base 50000, gated SiLU MLP, concat adapter for (s, e) concatenated, truncated backprop through the unrolled loop. At test time you can unroll further than training — that is the whole test-time scaling axis.

FLOPs multiplier~16× core
core repeats; prelude + coda run once
Paper pattern (illustrative)reasoning ↑↑ · facts →
GSM8k-style tasks keep gaining to 50×+; OpenBookQA-style flatten fast. Fig. 1.

Appendix B · Your cost question — answered

You asked: layering an AI on top of Perchance's LLM still costs more, right, since the base model doesn't follow this model? Yes. Exactly right.

True latent recurrence (Huginn)Our emulation below (layering)
Extra thinkingrepeat block R inside one forward pass — pure FLOPs, zero new tokensextra generateText calls — each one re-reads tokens + writes tokens
Context windowuntouched — all compute stays in vectorsevery refinement round-trip burns context and latency
What it provesreal scaling to ~50B-equivalent behavioronly the shape of the tradeoff: harder questions benefit from more rounds, easy facts don't

Rule of thumb for the demo below: K hidden rounds ≈ K× the token bill and wall-clock time. The real model pays ≈K× FLOPs but 1× tokens. That gap is why the architecture matters — and why section 4 is honest emulation, not the thing itself.

Appendix F · Run it fully offline — laptop, cluster, or EKS

Sections 1–3 and 5 above are already pure static JS — no network at all. Section 4 now has two backends: tick force offline up there to rehearse the cluster build right here on this page.

  • Cluster (any static host): copy index.html + src/ to nginx / S3+CloudFront / any intranet box. Zero env vars, zero keys.
