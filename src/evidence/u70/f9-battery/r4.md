1. **Symmetry Breaking Failure**: Halving the global scalar without adjusting the learning rate or bias initialization risks "vanishing signal" at birth. If the network fails to break symmetry, it will collapse to a mean output. This doesn't prove "volume" as the cause of catastrophes; it proves the network is too quiet to function, creating a false-positive "PERSISTS" verdict.

2. **Linearity Trap**: Scaling all weights by 0.5 in a network with non-linearities (e.g., ReLU, Tanh) does not simply "lower the volume"; it shifts the operating point of every neuron. This is a structural change in activation dynamics, not a scalar volume adjustment.

3. **Confounded Mechanism**: By scaling the entire net, you confound the "loud encoder" hypothesis with the "weak decoder/bridge" reality. You cannot isolate birth volume if the entire signal chain is attenuated.
