1. **Causal Attribution Failure**: The proposal claims deltas will "attribute cleanly" because only `ctxw` changes. However, since `L_ctx` is a component of the total loss, increasing its weight changes the gradient landscape for the entire network during the 200-epoch train. This is a global optimization shift, not a modular parameter change; you cannot isolate the effect of `ctxw` from the resulting shift in the learned representations of `L_pred` and `L_dyn`.

2. **Training Instability**: Increasing `L_ctx` from 1.0 to 3.0 without adjusting the learning rate (3e-4) or implementing a loss-scaling schedule risks gradient explosion or premature convergence to a suboptimal local minimum, potentially breaking the "F2 EXACT" stability baseline.

3. **Evaluation Circularity**: Using the same "forcing cut" for both training and evaluation while increasing the weight of that specific cut creates a feedback loop that may inflate `ctxAcc` without improving actual z-content.
