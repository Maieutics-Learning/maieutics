# F6 barrage r5 (verbatim)

1. **Gradient Leakage/Instability:** Introducing a soft-teacher head $\text{CE}(\text{softmax}(W_{pt}q + b_{pt}), c)$ with a weight of $1.0$ creates a secondary, unconstrained gradient path into the encoder. Since $q$ is the soft posterior, this is functionally a supervised signal on the latent distribution. Without a corresponding penalty or normalization, this risks "overpowering" the KL-rep term or inducing sudden collapses in the $z$-space, potentially masking the "flip" mechanism rather than solving it.

2. **Hidden Redundancy:** This is a disguised `ctxw` increase. By providing a gradient path via $q$ (soft) instead of $z$ (hard), you are simply smoothing the teacher's influence. If the "lottery" is driven by initialization, a smoother gradient won't fix a bad seed; it will only accelerate the convergence of a "lucky" one, making the $N=20$ sample size statistically insufficient to claim a mechanism shift.
