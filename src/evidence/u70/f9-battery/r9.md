1. **Symmetry Starvation:** Halving the global scalar without adjusting the learning rate or architecture induces a "vanishing signal" risk. If the initial weights are too quiet, the network may fail to break symmetry, leading to pooled collapse. This isn't a "persistence" of the F4 curse, but a failure of the mechanism to ignite.

2. **Confounding Scale vs. Distribution:** The proposal targets "volume" but ignores that $\text{std}(\text{weights})$ is the driver. By scaling all draws by 0.5, you are not testing "loud voices" versus "quiet voices," but shifting the entire distribution's variance. This is a disguised sweep of the initialization variance, not a targeted probe of "catastrophe births."

3. **Interpretability Gap:** If seeds 2,6,7,8 still fail, you cannot distinguish between a "draw pattern" (topology) and a "threshold effect" (the scale was still too high/low).
