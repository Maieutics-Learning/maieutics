NEEDS-PATCH

1. **Bimodal Blindness / Variance Shrinkage**: The dual-bar requirement (Mean $\ge X$ AND Median $\ge X$) fails to penalize high-variance "lottery" distributions. A dataset with a massive outlier (lucky tail) and a failing center can still pass the Mean bar, while a tight cluster just above the threshold passes the Median bar. Because you require *both* to pass, you've created a "floor" but not a "consistency" check. This doesn't solve the lucky-tail problem; it just requires the tail to be so extreme that it drags a failing median up to 0.70, or for the median to be barely scraping by while the mean is inflated. It ignores the variance of the 40 runs, allowing a "unstable" system to be marked FIXED if the median is marginally above 0.70.
